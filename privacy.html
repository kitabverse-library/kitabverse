1. Overview

KitabVerse is a free public library website. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and what we DO NOT collect, as per IFLA Ethics, ISO 9001:2015 and Google Play Store requirements.

2. Data We DO NOT Collect

We DO NOT collect your name, phone number, email, address, location, photos, contacts, or payment details. No login / sign-up is required to browse books.

3. Data We Collect

a) Visitor Counter: Only an anonymous total count of visits (e.g. 00001) is stored locally to know library usage. It does not identify you.
b) Search: Your search words are used only on your device to show books. They are not sent to our server.
c) Preferences: Font size (A- / A / A+), Night Mode and Quote settings are saved only in your browser (localStorage).

4. Cookies

We use only essential local storage for font size and theme. No advertising cookies, no tracking cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

Our site may open Google Maps for directions and Wikimedia Commons for the National Anthem audio. Those services have their own privacy policies. We do not share your data with them.

6. Children Privacy

KitabVerse is safe for all ages (Everyone). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Data Safety - Google Play

Data Collected: None. Data Shared: None. Data Deleted: Nothing to delete as nothing is stored on server.

8. Your Rights

You can clear your browser data anytime to delete preferences. You can use the site without giving any personal information.

9. Changes To Policy

If this policy changes, the Last Updated Date and Version will be updated here. Continued use means you accept the new policy.
