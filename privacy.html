KitabVerse — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Draft • Effective Date: To Be Announced At Launch

1. Who We Are

KitabVerse — Library For All With Advanced Features. Read. Share. Connect.
Owner: Satish Puthran, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
Contact: Via KitabVerse Contact Page / GitHub.

2. Data We Collect

We collect NO personal data by default.
We do NOT collect: Name, Email, Phone, Location, Photos, Contacts.
Books you add, Borrow list, Wishlist are stored ONLY on your own device (localStorage) in your browser. They are NOT sent to our server.

3. Admin Preview

Admin Preview is locked by password. Password is stored as a one-way hash only, not in plain text. We never see your password.

4. Third Parties

We use GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub may collect basic technical logs (IP, browser) as per GitHub Privacy Policy. We use NO advertising SDK, NO analytics tracking, NO Facebook SDK.

5. Children

KitabVerse is a family library for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, because we collect no data at all. Parents can use the site with children safely.

6. Your Rights

You can clear your browser data anytime to delete your Borrow / Wishlist. You can contact us to ask questions about this policy.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and update this page.
